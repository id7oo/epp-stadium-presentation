Part 4 · The closing argument

What we would tell LCGPA

A stadium is a deadline, not a market. The credit system will be satisfied long before any Saudi capability exists, unless the policy counts people who passed an independent test and production net of imported inputs — and keeps counting them two years after handover. Fund one package, verify it, then scale.

In 2034 the stadium will be finished either way. The question this project answers is what else is still standing afterwards.

Separate from EPP · verify at source [13]

The wider localisation toolkit

LCGPA mechanisms: the mandatory products list; the national product price preference; local content weighting inside financial evaluation; preference for local SMEs.

Company programmes: IKTVA (Aramco) scores suppliers on in-Kingdom value added; Nusaned (SABIC) enables local manufacturing; MUSAHAMA (PIF) sets localisation at owner level.

Construction has no single IKTVA — that is the finding. Several public owners each set their own local-content schedule, so a contractor may face both an owner requirement and an EP obligation.

Appendix · for questions

The full recommendation, and how we would measure it

To LCGPA
  • Publish clearer credit-factor criteria, so a bidder can price the obligation before signing.
  • Enable joint training programmes across qualifying contracts.
  • Measure competence and real Saudi production separately from credits.
  • Require independent quality and cost assessment before scale-up.
To a supplier or contractor
  • Confirm buyer, policy version, imported amount and responsible EP party first.
  • Record the baseline before training starts.
  • Agree activity, rights, partner, cost, factor and milestones with LCGPA.
  • Document real work and independent test results.
  • Review retention and value — change or stop what fails.
During construction

Test pass rates, fewer defects, safe on-time handover.

After handover

Employment retained at 12 and 24 months; graduates training others.

Across future projects

Production net of imported inputs; repeat orders.
