## The story in one line per part

1. **Our case:** a World Cup stadium is a construction job in a sector worth 7.8% of the Saudi economy.
2. **Multiplier and leakage:** every riyal spent is re-spent, but about one in five leaks abroad; in our SAR 4.5bn scenario that is SAR 421m, mostly chemicals and metals. This is a first-round estimate, not the final GDP multiplier.
3. **EPP:** that leak creates an SAR 84m EP obligation; London 2012 shows the skills lesson.
4. **The academy proposal:** one shared academy turns the obligation into Saudi capability that keeps working after the stadium opens. The credit factors are group assumptions within the draft ranges, subject to LCGPA approval.
